# PRD Registry

This table is the authoritative roadmap registry. It records capability-level intent only; detailed PRDs are created just in time under [PRD governance](README.md). Dependencies refer to PRD IDs and must be `COMPLETED` before a dependent PRD begins unless an approved detailed PRD explicitly identifies a non-runtime research lane permitted by the [Master Execution Plan](../execution/MASTER_EXECUTION_PLAN.md).

**Authorization rule:** `PROPOSED` is not authorization. PRDs 01–24 are `APPROVED` under the founder's Autonomous Pilot V1 authorization and may execute only when their dependencies and applicable gates are satisfied. PRD 25 remains `PROPOSED` and is not authorized.

**Approval basis:** Founder authorization — Autonomous Pilot V1 execution through Pilot Release Candidate. This approves the capability scope already represented by PRDs 01–24 and the roadmap; it does not authorize silent scope expansion or Pilot Release.

## Conditional Form Intelligence relationship

PRD 22 is not an unconditional dependency of PRD 24. The Pilot Release Candidate may proceed without Form Intelligence. If Form Intelligence is proposed for inclusion, PRD 22 must first pass its POC gate without weakened thresholds, and an explicitly approved integration scope must add the validated capability to the candidate's dependency and release-gate record.

## Pilot boundary

The target is the **Fitness OS Pilot Release Candidate** followed by an explicitly authorized Pilot Release. Native iOS and Apple Watch clients are excluded from Pilot V1. Their future inclusion requires a clear product reason and separately approved PRD under the PWA-first principle.
